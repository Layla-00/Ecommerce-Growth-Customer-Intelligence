# Business Questions

This analysis was designed to answer the following business questions:

1. Where are customers dropping off in the e-commerce conversion funnel?

2. Which products and customer segments contribute most to revenue?

3. How significant are cancelled and returned orders?

4. Which products show unusually high order failure rates?

5. Is there an observable relationship between product ratings and order failure?

6. Which findings represent the most actionable opportunities for improving growth and customer experience?
