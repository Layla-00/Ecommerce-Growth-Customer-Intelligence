# Key Findings

## Executive Performance

- Total Revenue: $11.92M
- Total Orders: 20,000
- Average Order Value: $595.93

## Conversion Funnel

- 70.21% of users who viewed products added items to cart.
- 53.09% of users who added items to cart did not complete a purchase.

## Order Performance

- Approximately 40% of orders were either cancelled or returned.
- Cancelled orders represented 19.60%.
- Returned orders represented 20.33%.

## Product & Customer Experience

- Several products showed failure rates between 65% and 70%.
- Astra Hundred had the lowest average rating among the highlighted high-failure products at 3.55/5.

## Key Business Insight

The analysis suggests that the largest opportunities are not limited to acquiring more customers. Improving cart conversion and investigating high-failure products may have a significant impact on revenue efficiency and customer experience.
